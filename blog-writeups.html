What’s in here

Each challenge gets its own folder with a short write-up and a runnable solution. The goal isn’t just the flag — it’s a clear record of how the challenge fell: the observation that mattered, the vulnerability or math trick behind it, and reproducible code so the solve can be followed step by step.
